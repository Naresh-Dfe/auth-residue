# Community conduct

Participants should be respectful, constructive, and considerate across experience levels and backgrounds. Critique ideas and code with evidence. Welcome questions and corrections. Respect privacy and the boundaries of authorized testing.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, impersonation, and sharing others' confidential data are unacceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, and project spaces. Do not use test fixtures to target other people or systems without permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, warn participants, restrict participation, or ban repeated or severe abuse. They should explain decisions when safe, protect reporters' privacy, and evaluate appeals impartially. Conflicts involving a maintainer should be reviewed by another available maintainer.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through their published contact channel. If no private channel is listed, ask for one without disclosing private allegations in a public issue. Maintainers should make that channel available before opening community discussions.
